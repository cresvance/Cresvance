Digital Nomad Visa · Spain

Live & work in Spain. Legally.

Spain's Digital Nomad Visa opens the door to a new way of living. Our advisory team guides you through every requirement, so you can focus on the move.

We handle the process with you from start to finish, whether you apply from your home country or already from within Spain.

Who qualifies

Remote workers employed under a foreign contract or W-2, freelancers with international clients, and self-employed professionals earning above Spain's income threshold.

Document preparation

We guide you through every required document: contracts, apostilles, criminal records, health insurance and more.

Consulate or in-country

Apply from your home consulate before arriving, or switch status from inside Spain. We support both routes.

Beckham Law tax benefit

Pay a flat 24% income tax rate instead of up to 47% for the first 6 years. We handle the application for you.

Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV)

Not working remotely but want to live in Spain on passive income or savings? We assess your eligibility and manage the full application.

Renewal support

We track your renewal deadlines and handle the entire process proactively, so you never have to worry about lapsing status.
